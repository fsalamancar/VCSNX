# VCSNX 0.1.0

The first public launch: GTA: Vice City Stories (PSP) running natively on the Nintendo Switch, with Switch
controls and prompts, a smaller HUD and the bAlo splash.

- **`vcsnx.nro`**: the runtime. It contains nothing of the game.
- **`VCSNX-Builder-0.1.0.zip`** (Windows 10/11, 64-bit): the "GTA VCS Compiler" window makes **`vcs_game.so`**,
  the game's code, on your PC from **your own copy** of the game (ULUS-10160, US): the `PSP_DATA` folder of
  the VCS PC port ([VCSNative](https://github.com/OverkillLabs3/vice-city-stories-recomp)). It can copy
  everything to your SD card. See `README.txt` inside the zip. No game files are included.
